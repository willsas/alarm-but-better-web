Common questions

My alarm didn't ring

Work through these in order:

  1. Open Settings → Alarm but better and check that permission to schedule alarms is granted.
  2. In the app, confirm the alarm's toggle is on and the repeat days include today.
  3. Check the phone was charged and switched on at the alarm time.
  4. Try toggling the alarm off and on again — this re-registers it with iOS.

Still wrong? Email us with the alarm time, its repeat days and your iOS version.

Does it work in silent mode or Focus?

Yes. The app uses Apple's AlarmKit, the same system the built-in Clock app uses, so alarms sound through silent mode and Focus. That's also why iOS asks for a separate permission the first time.

I paid for Premium but it's locked

Open the Premium screen (or Settings inside the app) and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. If it still doesn't unlock, email us — please don't buy it twice.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions, choose Alarm but better and tap Cancel. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep Premium until the end of the period you've paid for. We can't cancel it on your behalf — Apple handles all billing.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We have no ability to issue them.

Why doesn't my theme animate on the Lock Screen?

Live themes and Live Photo wallpapers animate inside the app. On the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, iOS renders a Live Activity as a still image — that's a system limitation, not a bug. Those themes fall back to their gradient and decoration there, which is why every live theme also defines a static look.

My alarm still shows the old theme

iOS captures an alarm's appearance when it's scheduled. Changing a theme updates alarms that are simply waiting, but an alarm that is currently ringing or counting down keeps its old look until it next rings. The app tells you when this happens.

Will it sync between my devices?

Not currently. Alarms and themes are stored only on the device that created them, because the app has no servers and collects no data.

Do you collect any of my data?

No. No accounts, no analytics, no networking code at all. See the Privacy Policy.
