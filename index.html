Alarms worth waking up to

A real alarm clock for iPhone — it rings through silent mode and Focus, just like the built-in Clock. The difference is that it looks like something you chose.

Download on the App Store

Requires iPhone with iOS 26 or later · Free to download

What it does

Real alarms

Built on Apple's AlarmKit, so alarms break through silent mode and Focus — not notifications that get swallowed.

Themes everywhere

Your theme follows you to the Lock Screen, the Dynamic Island and StandBy — not just inside the app.

Make your own

Your colours, your photo or Live Photo, your glyph, your motion. Build a theme nobody else has.

Sounds you pick

Choose a tone, import an audio file, or record your own voice as the alarm.

Timers, full screen

Prop the phone up while you cook. Digital or a proper stopwatch face, wearing the same theme.

Home Screen widgets

Next alarm, quick timers and a themed clock, in every widget size.

A look at it

Every screen wears the theme you pick — typeface, cards and all.
