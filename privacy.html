Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 August 2026

The short version. Alarm but better does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. The app contains no networking code — everything you create stays in the app's private storage on your iPhone.

1. Who we are

This policy covers the iOS app Alarm but better ("the app") and this website. In this policy, "we" and "us" refer to the developer of the app. You can reach us at hello@alarmbutbetter.com.

2. Data we collect

None. We do not collect, receive or store any personal data about you. We have no servers that the app talks to, and the app is built without any network, analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code.

3. Data the app stores on your device

The app saves what you create so it can work. All of it is kept in the app's own private storage (an iOS App Group container shared only between the app and its widgets) and none of it is transmitted anywhere.

WhatWhyWhere
Alarms and timers To schedule and display them On device
Themes you create To render your chosen look On device
Photos or Live Photos you pick as a wallpaper To draw behind your alarm On device, copied and resized
Audio you import or record To play as an alarm sound On device
App settings To remember your preferences On device

Deleting the app removes all of it. We have no copy and no way to recover it.

4. Permissions the app asks for

Alarms and timers (AlarmKit)

Required for the app's core purpose. iOS asks for this so alarms can ring through silent mode and Focus. It grants no access to personal information.

Microphone

Requested only if you choose to record your own alarm sound. The recording is written straight to the app's private storage and used solely as an alarm tone. It is never uploaded, analysed or shared. If you never use that feature, the app never asks.

Photos

The app uses Apple's system photo picker. That means the app does not get access to your photo library — iOS only hands over the single item you select. The app then makes a resized copy in its own storage for use as a wallpaper.

5. Purchases

Premium subscriptions are sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the payment; we never see or receive your payment details, card number, billing address or Apple Account.

The app asks iOS whether a valid subscription exists, and iOS answers yes or no. That check happens between your device and Apple. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Apple may provide us with anonymised, aggregated sales and subscription figures in App Store Connect. These cannot identify you.

6. Third parties

The app includes no third-party SDKs, trackers, advertising networks or analytics services. No data is shared with anyone, because none is collected.

7. This website

This site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads no third-party fonts, scripts or images. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and reliability; we do not use these to identify visitors.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, including children.

9. Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export or delete personal data a company holds about you. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, export or delete. Data stored on your device is entirely under your control and is removed when you delete the app.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the date at the top. Material changes affecting how data is handled will also be noted in the app's release notes.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@alarmbutbetter.com and we'll reply.
